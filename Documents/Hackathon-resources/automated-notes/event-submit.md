## Before you submit (as shown on the form)

- "Read the full rules before you file this. Every submission must include:"
  - A public source-code repository
  - A README with setup instructions and your graph data model
  - The Cypher queries or graph algorithms the product depends on
  - A demo video showing the working project
  - A live deployment, or complete local setup instructions if it cannot be deployed
  - The track you are entering

## Part 1 of 2 — Project Details (6 fields)

- **Project title** — required.
- **Description** — required; prompt: "What problem does your project solve, and who is it for?"
- **What track are you submitting for?** — required; "Select all applicable tracks":
  - Agents That Act on Connected Data
  - Agent Memory and Coordination
  - Company Brain
- **Repository** — required; "This is what your project gets listed and opened by."
- **Demo video** — required; "It should be a YouTube video demo link. Keep it public or unlisted, so we can access the video through the link."
- **Live demo or deployment** — optional field (not marked required).
- **How did you use FalkorDB in your project?** — required; "Tell us what technical problems you solved using FalkorDB."
- **Blog link** — optional; "Write a blog post about your project, the problem you solve and your learnings, and share it here. The best blog post wins Apple AirPods 5."

## Part 2 of 2 — Feedback (2 fields)

- **What did you like about FalkorDB?** — required.
- **What improvements would you like to see in FalkorDB?** — required.

## Key facts and numbers

- Total questions: **8** (6 project + 2 feedback).
- Required fields: title, description, track(s), repository, demo video, how you used FalkorDB, both feedback answers — **7 required**, plus the optional live demo and blog link.
- Demo video format: **YouTube link, public or unlisted**.
- Editing: submissions **remain editable until the deadline** (Oct 18, 11:59 PM IST).
- Side prize hook: **blog link → best blog post wins Apple AirPods 5**.
- Track field is multi-select ("Select all applicable tracks").

## Hackathon relevance

- This is the exact form to prepare against: have the repo public, README complete (setup + graph model), queries/algorithms listed, a YouTube demo video up, and the FalkorDB write-up ready before filling it in.
- The "How did you use FalkorDB in your project?" answer is effectively the removal-test pitch (rule 3) — prepare it as a short technical narrative.
- The feedback questions are required; answer them thoughtfully, and still include the blog link — it is a separate side prize (AirPods 5).
- Submit early and edit until the deadline — the form itself says editing is allowed through the end.
