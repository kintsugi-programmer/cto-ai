Next, you'll see that you can also control different facets of your instance and tailor it to your exact needs. For example, you can assign the amount of memory that you need, a name, and a description for your instance, as well as the primary user and his associated credentials right here from this panel. In contrast to the Faler DB cloud free tier, the startup tier allows you to toggle TLS on and off, tune the persistence, and control a variety of parameters that will suit your use case. When you're done, hit create to spin up the instance. Click on the instances name to appear inside it. You'll find important information like your instance ID or the database's endpoint. With both saved for later in the configuration, you're ready to connect and visualize your data. Once your instance is live, you can hit the connect button, which will bring up FalDB's graph visualization tool called browser. You'll see that the endpoint, port, and username are already pre-filled. All you have to do is enter your password. As you can see, the default query the browser ran visualizes the data that's currently available. And zooming in on a node and then right-clicking it will bring up the editable attributes panel, which allows you to consult or modify individual nodes at any time. Clicking on a specific node or edge inside the graph info panel on the left acts as a smart filter. It allows you to filter graph data points from the point of view of the specific edge type. For example, filtering by route would simply display the possible ways to travel from one airport to the other by understanding how the two are connected. You can also view the query performance in a query history window.
